Frequently asked questions

What can I do with Quotation Assistant?

Quotation Assistant is a quotation management app for iOS 17 or later. Organize customer and product information, create and edit quotations, and export them as PDFs to share.

How do I create a quotation?

Create a quotation in the app, enter customer details, and add line items. Review quantities, unit prices, and totals, then export a PDF. Check customer details, amounts, and terms before sending.

What if PDF export or sharing fails?

Check that your quotation is complete and your device has available storage. Reopen the app and try exporting again, or save the PDF to Files before sharing. If the issue persists, email support with the steps you took and a screenshot of the error.

What if my data looks wrong or the app will not open?

Try closing and reopening the app and check for an app update. To avoid losing local information, contact support before uninstalling the app or clearing its data. Include your device model, iOS version, and what you were doing before the issue occurred.

What if my purchase or membership is missing?

Check your internet connection and confirm you are using the Apple Account used for the purchase. Try restoring purchases if the app offers that option. If your purchase is still missing, contact support with relevant order information, redacting payment details and other sensitive information.

What should I include in a support request?

Describe what you expected, what happened, and the steps to reproduce the issue. Include your app version, iOS version, and device model. Redact customer names, contact details, confidential quotations, and other sensitive information from screenshots. Never send passwords, verification codes, or payment card information.
